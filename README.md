## ◉ THE VISION

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://readme-typing-svg.demolab.com?font=Fira+Code&weight=600&size=18&duration=2200&pause=600&color=A78BFA&center=true&vCenter=true&width=800&height=50&lines=What+if+health+information+felt+simpler%3F;What+if+tracking+became+more+accessible%3F;What+if+technology+helped+us+make+informed+choices%3F" alt="Animated vision"/>
</p>

**DiaCare AI** is an early-stage healthcare technology prototype focused on exploring a more accessible way to understand blood glucose information, discover wellness resources, and encourage healthier habits.

The project currently begins with the fundamentals of web development. Its long-term vision is to evolve into a more capable, secure, and thoughtfully designed digital health platform.

> ⚡ **Built today with the basics. Imagined for tomorrow with intelligent technology.**

This repository represents an evolving prototype—not a completed medical platform. Features and technologies will be added progressively as development advances.

## ◉ THE PRINCIPLES BEHIND THE PROJECT

## ⚕️ A NOTE ON HEALTH & SAFETY

DiaCare AI is a prototype intended to explore health awareness and educational experiences. It is **not a medical device, diagnostic tool, or substitute for a qualified healthcare professional**.

The prototype must not be relied upon to diagnose diabetes, prescribe medication, or determine treatment changes. Any future health-related functionality should be appropriately tested and reviewed before real-world use.
